# Full Preliminary Research Proposal Template

## Before drafting

Confirm the output language first:

> 你要英文 RP 还是中文 RP？

If the user has not provided an advisor link, state that the advisor fit is provisional and based on the user's interest profile, CV, and target field.

## Title

Use a broad theoretical title plus a concrete empirical setting:

> [Concept 1], [Concept 2], and [Concept 3] in [broad setting]: Evidence from [observable case]

## 1. Introduction

Target: approximately 300-400 English words, or comparable focused Chinese prose.

- State the background, real or academic motivation, problem domain, and focal population, organization, platform, region, or phenomenon.
- End with the preliminary research question or objective.
- Do not begin with a story, news lead, or autobiography.
- Cite every externally supported claim.

## 2. Literature Review

Target: approximately 600-800 English words, or comparable focused Chinese prose.

- Organize the review by themes or debates rather than listing papers one by one.
- Cover prior questions, data, methods, and main findings.
- Compare method strengths and weaknesses.
- Normally use 10-15 verified sources, including reviews, seminal theory, recent empirical work, and relevant advisor publications.
- End explicitly with statements such as:
  - "Existing studies have paid limited attention to..."
  - "Previous research has not fully explained..."
  - "Comparative evidence on ... remains limited."
- Do not claim that nobody has studied the topic unless the search was systematic and the claim is supportable.

## 3. Theoretical Framework

Target: approximately 400-600 English words, or comparable focused Chinese prose.

- Select one main theory, model, or school.
- Name its originator or foundational source.
- Explain important applications and the concepts that matter for this proposal.
- Explain why it fits the research setting.
- Map theory -> variables -> research questions.
- Do not introduce several theories without using them in the design.

## 4. Research Questions and Objectives

Target: approximately 300 English words or less, or a comparable concise Chinese section.

Write one answerable main question:

> How does [observable feature] relate to [observable outcome] under [context]?

Add 2-4 sub-questions or hypotheses. At least one should specify a comparison. Use `RQ1`, `RQ2`, or `H1`, `H2` consistently.

## 5. Methodology

Target: approximately 500-700 English words when the overall page limit permits.

State:

- research type: qualitative, quantitative, mixed methods, case study, or other;
- population and sample;
- sample selection rule;
- data sources and their access status;
- unit of analysis;
- variables and operational definitions;
- comparison groups;
- analysis methods and software;
- first practical implementation step;
- feasibility based on the user's skills and available resources.

For public data, explain how to:

- acquire a test batch;
- preserve raw data;
- create a data dictionary;
- clean and standardize identifiers;
- join sources;
- manually validate a sample;
- run the first descriptive statistic or visualization.

Include validation and limitations:

- measurement checks;
- alternative operationalizations;
- time ordering;
- reverse causality;
- selection and survivorship;
- missing data;
- ethics and reproducibility.

Use "associated with" for observational results unless a causal identification strategy is actually specified.

## 6. Expected Contribution

Separate the expected:

- theoretical contribution;
- empirical contribution;
- methodological contribution;
- practical or policy contribution.

State what the study could newly compare, measure, or explain. Avoid generic claims such as "enrich the literature."

## 7. References

Use APA 7th or the format requested by the user. Every reference must include:

- exact title;
- authors;
- venue and year;
- DOI or real stable link;
- publication status when not a standard journal article.

Keep original publication titles. A Chinese translation may be added in parentheses for readability, but must not replace the verified title or be presented as the official title.

For data and software documentation, cite the official organization and link separately from academic references.
